# Kirigami Delegates QML Types

Kirigami Delegates.

- [CheckSubtitleDelegate](qml-org-kde-kirigami-delegates-checksubtitledelegate.md): A convenience wrapper combining QtQuick Controls CheckDelegate and IconTitleSubtitle.
- [IconTitleSubtitle](qml-org-kde-kirigami-delegates-icontitlesubtitle.md): A simple item containing an icon, title and subtitle.
- [RadioSubtitleDelegate](qml-org-kde-kirigami-delegates-radiosubtitledelegate.md): A convenience wrapper combining QtQuick Controls RadioDelegate and IconTitleSubtitle.
- [SubtitleDelegate](qml-org-kde-kirigami-delegates-subtitledelegate.md): A convenience wrapper combining QtQuick Controls ItemDelegate and IconTitleSubtitle.
- [SwitchSubtitleDelegate](qml-org-kde-kirigami-delegates-switchsubtitledelegate.md): A convenience wrapper combining QtQuick Controls SwitchDelegate and IconTitleSubtitle.
- [TitleSubtitle](qml-org-kde-kirigami-delegates-titlesubtitle.md): A simple item containing a title and subtitle label.
- [TitleSubtitleWithActions](qml-org-kde-kirigami-delegates-titlesubtitlewithactions.md): A simple title delegate that has trailing actions.
