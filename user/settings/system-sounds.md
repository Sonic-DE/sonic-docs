# System Sounds

This module, under **Appearance & Style** > **Colors & Themes**, chooses the sound theme: the set of sounds the desktop and applications play for notifications, such as a new message, a warning, a low battery or a device being plugged in.

SonicDE comes with the **Ocean** sound theme, which is used unless you choose another, and the **Oxygen** sound theme.

## Choosing a sound theme

Each installed theme is shown as a card with its name and description. If a theme builds on other themes, the card says **Based on:** with their names; sounds the theme doesn't have come from those themes.

Click a card to select the theme, then click **Apply**. Double-clicking a card applies it at once.

## Listening to a theme

Next to **Preview:** on each card are buttons that play a sample of the theme's sounds: the window system bell, a warning, a new message, a low battery and a device being added. Hover over a button to see which sound it plays. The large play button on the right of the card plays the theme's demo sound. Click a button again to stop the sound.

If the theme doesn't have a sound you try to play, the module says so.

## Turning notification sounds off

**Enable notification sounds** at the top turns all notification sounds on or off. It's on by default.

To silence one application or event instead, use [Notifications](notifications.md).

## Installing themes

**Install from File…** installs a sound theme you've downloaded, from a `.tar.gz`, `.tar.bz2`, `.tar.xz`, `.tar.lz` or `.zip` file. You can also drop the file onto the module.
