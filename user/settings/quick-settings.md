# Quick Settings

Quick Settings is the page that appears when System Settings starts. It gathers a few settings that people change often, and shortcuts to the pages you use most.

## Theme

**Theme:** shows three choices:

- A light global theme. By default this is **Silver Light - Bottom Panel**.
- A dark global theme. By default this is **Silver Dark - Bottom Panel**.
- **Automatic:** Uses the light theme during the day and the dark theme at night. Hover over it to see which two themes it switches between.

Click a picture to use that theme. To use a different light or dark theme, click the arrow button next to the picture, **Change global theme**, and choose another one.

**More appearance settings:** has buttons that open the [Wallpaper](wallpaper.md) settings and the [Global Theme](global-theme.md) module.

## Animation speed

**Animation speed:** A slider from **Slow** to **Instant** that sets how fast animations in the desktop and applications run. **Instant** turns animations off.

## Clicking files or folders

**Clicking files or folders:**

- **Selects them:** A single click selects, and a double click opens. This is the default.
- **Opens them:** A single click opens. To select, click the item's selection marker.

This is the same setting as in [General Behavior](general-behavior.md). **More behavior settings:** opens that module.

## Most used pages

**Most used pages:** shows buttons for the System Settings modules you open most. Click one to go to that module. It only appears once there's something to show.
