# Wallpaper

This module, under **Appearance & Style**, sets the picture or other background behind your desktop. It has the same wallpaper settings you get by right-clicking the desktop and choosing **Desktop and Wallpaper**.

## Choosing a wallpaper type

**Wallpaper type:** chooses how the background is drawn. The settings for the chosen type appear below it. SonicDE comes with these types:

- **Image:** A single picture. This is the default type.
- **Slideshow:** Pictures from folders you choose, changing from time to time.
- **Plain Color:** One color.
- **Picture of the Day:** A new picture from the Internet each day.
- **Tiled:** A small picture repeated to fill the screen.
- **Haenau:** An animated wallpaper.
- **Hunyango:** A patterned wallpaper on a colored background. **Randomize Wallpaper Color** in the desktop's right-click menu picks a new color.

**Get New Plugins…** next to the list downloads more wallpaper types, which needs an Internet connection.

## Several screens

With more than one screen connected, the module shows **Set for all screens** at the top:

- When it's on, the wallpaper you choose is used on every screen.
- When it's off, a picture of your screens appears. Click a screen to choose the wallpaper for that screen only.

Click **Apply** to use your changes.
