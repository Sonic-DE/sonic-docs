# Wallpapers

To change your desktop background, right-click the desktop and choose **Desktop and Wallpaper**. On the **Wallpaper** page, **Wallpaper type:** chooses what kind of background you get. This page covers the basic types:

- **Image:** One picture.
- **Slideshow:** Pictures from one or more folders, changing every so often.
- **Plain Color:** A single color.

SonicDE also comes with **Picture of the Day**, **Tiled**, **Haenau** and **Hunyango**, described in [More wallpaper types](more-wallpapers.md). **Get New Plugins…** finds more on the Internet.

## Image

Pick a picture from the grid. In the **Images** heading above the grid:

- **Add…** adds a picture from your computer to the grid.
- **Get New…** downloads more wallpapers from the Internet.

Hover over a picture for more buttons: **Open Containing Folder** shows where the picture is stored, and **Remove Wallpaper** takes a picture you added out of the grid. Until you apply the change, **Restore wallpaper** brings it back.

These settings change how the picture is shown:

- **Positioning:** **Scaled and cropped** fills the screen and cuts off what doesn't fit. **Scaled** stretches the picture to the screen. **Scaled, keep proportions** fits the whole picture on the screen without stretching it. **Centered** shows the picture at its own size in the middle of the screen.
- **Background:** When the picture doesn't cover the whole screen (with **Scaled, keep proportions** or **Centered**), this fills the rest: **Blur** uses a blurred copy of the picture, and **Solid color** uses a color you pick.
- **Switch dynamic wallpapers:** Some wallpapers come in a light and a dark variant. Choose when to use which: **Based on whether the desktop style is light or dark**, **Based on the day-night cycle**, **Always use light variant** or **Always use dark variant**. With the day-night cycle, **Configure…** opens the Day-Night Cycle module, where you set when day and night begin.

## Slideshow

A slideshow has the same **Positioning** and **Background** settings as a single image, plus:

- **Order:** **Random**, **A to Z**, **Z to A**, **Date modified (newest first)** or **Date modified (oldest first)**. **Group by folders** keeps the pictures of each folder together.
- **Change every:** How long each picture stays, in hours, minutes and seconds.
- **Folders:** The folders the pictures come from. **Add…** adds a folder, **Remove Folder** removes one, and **Open Folder…** opens it in your file manager.

The grid shows all the pictures in those folders. Uncheck a picture to leave it out of the slideshow. **Select All** and **Select None** check or uncheck all of them at once.

## Plain Color

**Color:** opens a color picker for the background color.
