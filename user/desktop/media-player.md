# Media Player

The Media Player widget controls the applications that play music or video, such as a music player or a video in your web browser. It sits in the [System Tray](system-tray.md), and you can also add it to the desktop or a panel. Hover over its icon to see what's playing and by which artist.

## Quick controls on the icon

- **Middle-click** the icon to pause, or to play again.
- **Scroll** over the icon to change the player's volume.

## The pop-up

Click the icon to open the pop-up. It shows the cover art and what's playing, with a progress bar you can drag to jump within the track. Below are the controls: **Shuffle**, **Previous Track**, **Play** or **Pause**, **Next Track** and **Repeat**. Clicking the repeat button cycles through repeating the whole list (**Repeat**), repeating only the current track (**Repeat Track**) and not repeating. Buttons the player doesn't support are greyed out.

If the player lets you change the playback speed, the **Playback rate** button opens a menu of speeds. When the speed isn't normal, the button shows it, for example "1.5x".

When more than one application is playing, tabs at the top of the pop-up let you choose which one to control. **Choose player automatically** follows whichever player is active.

## Context menu

Right-click the icon for these entries:

- **Open:** Brings the player's window to the front, or opens it.
- **Previous Track**, **Play** or **Pause**, **Next Track** and **Stop**.
- **Quit:** Closes the player.
