# More wallpaper types

Besides a single image, a slideshow and a plain color, SonicDE has a few more kinds of wallpaper. To use one, right-click the desktop and choose **Desktop and Wallpaper** (or press Ctrl+Shift+D), then pick it from **Wallpaper type:**. Its settings appear below.

## Picture of the Day

Shows a new picture from the Internet every day. The settings are:

- **Positioning:** How the picture fits the screen: **Scaled and cropped**, **Scaled**, **Scaled, keep proportions**, **Centered** or **Tiled**.
- **Background color:** The color around the picture when it doesn't fill the screen. The default is black.
- **Provider:** Where the pictures come from: **Astronomy (NASA)** (the default), **Bing**, **Flickr**, **Simon Stålenhag Wallpapers** or **Wikimedia Commons**. For Wikimedia Commons, a help button warns that the provider doesn't filter out images that may be sensitive or objectionable, so take care if your screen is seen in public.
- **Update when using metered network connection:** Lets the wallpaper download a new picture while you're on a connection marked as metered, such as a phone's mobile data. It's off by default, and only appears when SonicDE can tell what kind of connection you have.

Below these, the settings show **Today's picture:** with its **Title:** and **Author:** when the provider gives them. The preview has buttons to **Save Image as…** on your computer and to **Open Link in Browser…** for the picture's web page. After you save the picture, **Open Containing Folder** shows where it went.

## Tiled

Repeats one small image across the whole desktop, like tiles on a floor. Click **Select Image…** next to **Tile:** to choose the image. Once you have chosen one, the button shows it, and clicking it chooses another.

## Haenau and Hunyango

Two drawn wallpapers with no settings:

- **Haenau** is a picture made of layers, which slowly fade and change, one layer every 30 seconds.
- **Hunyango** is a pattern on a background color that's chosen at random. Right-click the desktop and choose **Randomize Wallpaper Color** to pick a new one.
