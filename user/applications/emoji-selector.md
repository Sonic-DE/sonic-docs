# Emoji Selector

Emoji Selector lists every emoji, so you can find one and copy it. Paste it wherever you want it, with Ctrl+V or your application's Paste command.

Open it with Meta+. (Meta and the full stop), from the application launcher, or by running `plasma-emojier`. Meta+Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Space opens it too.

## Finding an emoji

The sidebar has these pages:

- **Recent:** the emoji you've used lately.
- **All:** every emoji.
- One page for each category, such as smileys, animals or food.

Type in the search field at the top of a page to filter the emoji by name. Hover over an emoji to see its name. Esc clears the search, or closes Emoji Selector when the search field is empty.

## Copying an emoji

Click an emoji, or select it with the keyboard and press Enter, to copy it to the clipboard. A short message says it was copied, and the emoji is added to **Recent**.

Right-click an emoji, or touch and hold it, for:

- **Copy Character:** copies the emoji.
- **Copy Description:** copies its name instead.

## Skin tones

**Skin tone:**, at the top of the **All** page and of the people category, chooses the skin tone for emoji that have one: **Neutral**, **Light**, **Medium Light**, **Medium**, **Medium Dark** or **Dark**.

Some emoji of two people can show a different skin tone for each person. When you hover over one of these, a small button appears on it. Click it to open **Select Variant** and pick the combination you want.

## Clearing the history

On the **Recent** page, **Clear History** empties the list of recently used emoji.
