# Getting new add-ons

Many settings modules and applications have a **Get New…** button, for example for [icons](../settings/icons.md), [cursors](../settings/pointers.md), [global themes](../settings/global-theme.md) and wallpapers. It opens a window that lists add-ons that other people have shared online. The window looks the same everywhere; only the kind of add-on changes.

> **Warning:** Add-ons are made by other users. A message at the top of the window reminds you that they haven't been tested for quality, and, for kinds of add-ons that can contain programs or scripts, that they haven't been tested for safety either. Only install add-ons you trust.

## Finding add-ons

Along the top:

- **All**, **Installed** or **Updateable**: Shows every add-on, only the ones you've installed, or only installed ones that have a newer version.
- **Sort:** Orders the list by **Release date**, **Name**, **Rating** or **Downloads**.
- **Search…** (Ctrl+F): Finds add-ons by name or description. Press Enter to search.

If there are several kinds of add-ons, **Category:** at the bottom of the window narrows the list to one of them.

## Installing, updating and removing

Each add-on in the list has buttons to install it, or, once installed, to **Update** it when there's a newer version, or **Uninstall** it.

Click an add-on to open its details page, with a description, pictures, its **Rating:** and links to its **Homepage:** and **How To Donate:**. The details page has the same buttons. **See … Reviews and Comments** opens what other people have written about it; choose **Show All Comments**, **Show Reviews Only** or **Show Reviews and Replies**.

When an add-on comes in several files, such as different sizes or variants, the button is **Install…**. It opens **Pick Your Installation Option**, where you choose which one to install.

## Sharing your own

When the add-on store accepts new entries, **Contribute Your Own…** at the bottom of the window, or **Upload…** in the toolbar, explains how to share your own add-ons and links to the store's website.
